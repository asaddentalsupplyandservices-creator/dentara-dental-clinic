About Dentara Studio

One clinic, two disciplines, a single standard of care.

Dentara Studio is a modern dental and aesthetic care clinic in PCSIR Phase 2, Lahore. We were founded on a simple idea: the way you care for your smile and the way you care for your skin shouldn't require two different clinics, two different standards, or two different teams.

Every treatment is planned and delivered under the direct guidance of Dr. Munazza Yasmeen, BDS, RDS, who brings over six years of hands-on experience across dentistry and non-surgical aesthetics — so your care plan is coordinated, not fragmented.
